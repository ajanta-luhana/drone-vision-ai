# Inspection and Reporting Procedures

## Mission inspection procedure
Operators start a mission, upload or stream footage, and monitor alerts. Analysts search historical incidents and generate mission reports.

## Incident review
Every HIGH or CRITICAL incident requires an operator decision: approve (confirm), dismiss, or investigate. Dismissals need a short comment so that feedback can be used to evaluate false positives.

## Reporting
Reports are generated as PDF, JSON and CSV. Reports contain mission information, incident information, location, timestamps, detected objects, AI analysis, risk assessment, evidence, operator decision and model version.

## Data retention and privacy
Footage is access controlled. Evidence frames are retained only as long as the incident is open plus the retention period. Do not store personally identifying imagery unnecessarily; face and licence plate blurring is planned.
