# Vehicle Entering Restricted Zone - SOP

## Procedure for a vehicle entering Restricted Zone A
1. The operator acknowledges the alert and opens the incident details page.
2. Verify the evidence frame and the track trajectory. Confirm the object is really a vehicle and is inside the polygon.
3. Check whether the vehicle is stationary. A vehicle stationary longer than the configured threshold raises risk.
4. Check whether a person approached the vehicle (person-vehicle proximity).
5. Contact on-site security to physically investigate. The platform never dispatches or controls anything autonomously.
6. Record the decision as confirmed, dismissed, or investigating, and add a comment. The decision is stored in the audit log.
7. Generate the incident report for HIGH and CRITICAL incidents.

## Escalation
CRITICAL incidents (risk 76 to 100) must be escalated to the shift supervisor within 5 minutes.
