# Zone Rules

## Restricted Zone
A Restricted Zone is an area where no unauthorised person or vehicle may enter. Any object entering a Restricted Zone generates a ZONE_INTRUSION event with HIGH base risk. Restricted Zone A covers the secured compound perimeter.

## Inspection Zone
Inspection Zones permit authorised inspection crews only. Entry is logged and scored at low risk unless the object remains longer than the permitted dwell time.

## Emergency Zone
Emergency Zones must stay clear for emergency vehicles. Any stationary vehicle in an Emergency Zone must be reviewed by an operator immediately.

## Construction Zone
Construction Zones allow construction equipment and authorised workers. Other vehicles entering are flagged at reduced risk.

## Monitoring Zone
Monitoring Zones are observed only. Entries are recorded for analytics and do not create alerts on their own.
